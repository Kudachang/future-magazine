# FUTURE™ MAGAZINE

**Status:** Ongoing exploration and experiment — not a finalized publication, brand system, or business plan.  
**Parent brand:** FUTURE™  
**Founder / Creative Director / Editor:** Bill Ho  
**Core principle:** **AI handles the work. Bill handles the taste.**

## Why this exists

FUTURE™ is a multidimensional, one-person creative studio. Creativity can include creating films, products and ideas—but also discovering, selecting, framing and publishing interesting work by others.

FUTURE MAGAZINE explores whether one creative director, supported by AI, can run a visually compelling, research-led digital magazine without the staffing and administration of a traditional media team.

The magazine should express **FUTURE™'s creative taste**, guided by Bill as its founder and Creative Director. It is **not** a personal diary or a feed limited to Bill's individual interests. Taste is the starting point; the publication develops a recognizable editorial identity of its own.

The experiment also aims to:
- Build awareness and exposure for **FUTURE™ and its founder** on Instagram, Xiaohongshu/Rednote, LinkedIn and possibly Threads.
- Demonstrate FUTURE™'s approach to AI-assisted creative direction and execution.
- Test whether AI can learn an evolving editorial standard through quick selections, rejections and occasional discussions.
- Explore future commercial possibilities, including partnerships and sponsorships, **without assuming an audience or revenue will materialize**.

**Long-term ambition:** A sustainable, low-maintenance media asset and a practical demonstration of a one-person, AI-enabled creative operation. Not yet proven.

## Editorial territory

**Creativity in unexpected places.** Not a traditional advertising trade magazine, a general-purpose culture feed, or a generic AI news account.

Discover unusual ideas, playful or provocative perspectives, inventive objects, unexpected technology applications, gaming culture, compelling AIGC, visual experiments, open creator ecosystems and striking work across fashion, design and entertainment.

What qualifies is **the idea and its editorial/visual potential**, not its industry category. Interesting does not have to mean viral, new or commercially successful. An older discovery can be featured if there is a reason to revisit it.

Working questions:
- Would someone stop scrolling?
- Is there a genuinely surprising idea or perspective?
- Can the visual communicate the hook quickly?
- Does investigation reveal something more interesting than the headline?
- Is the central factual claim verifiable?

These are **working criteria**, not locked rules.

## Editorial and production workflow

1. **Discover and verify.** ChatGPT prepares 18 distinct story candidates daily across a broad editorial range. For each, include a concise angle, a direct original-source URL, a visual or media link, the date, supporting sources where useful, and any verification gaps. Check dates, claims, and evidence of virality.
2. **Select.** Bill marks candidates **Keep / Pass / Maybe** and may bring his own discoveries. Carry selected stories into editorial development. Record meaningful editorial decisions and treat individual choices as feedback.
3. **Write and approve the copy together.** Bill and the AI collaborator (preferably ChatGPT for copywriting, or Codex) draft and refine two pieces for each selected story. The **headline** goes on the cover. The **body copy** goes in the social post caption. Save both in `01_EDITORIAL` for the issue and get Bill's approval on both before starting the mockup. Return any proposed headline changes to Bill for review.
4. **Hand off to Lovart.** Send the approved headline, visual direction, and image references or approved assets. Keep the post body copy in the editorial file. Use the working 1080 × 1350 DIN Pro grid, leave the lower-left logo area clear, and save Lovart exports in `02_LOVART_MOCKUPS`.
5. **Composite and review.** Apply the original transparent FUTURE™ logo using the contrast variant that fits the lower-left image area. Place the visible mark at x54, 205 px wide, with its bottom edge aligned to y1296. Check the headline, logo placement, and legibility at phone size. Save the finished artwork in `03_FINAL` and review it with Bill alongside the approved caption.
6. **Publish only with Bill's approval.** Publishing is a later-phase experiment and requires separate authorization for the platform and post.

The eventual goal is **eight strong stories a day**, but the current research output is 18 candidates. Production cadence and monetization remain open.

## Visual development — provisional

- **Candidate master carousel ratio:** **4:5 portrait (1080 × 1350 px)** for Instagram; evaluate platform-specific alternatives for Rednote and other channels. Earlier square and 3:4 possibilities were discussed but not selected.
- **Typography:** **DIN Pro**, consistent with FUTURE™'s existing visual work.
- **Identity:** Use Bill's **official FUTURE™ logo artwork**, never AI-invented replacements. The Mac production folder contains the original transparent black, white, and chrome-F cover variants.
- **Cover:** Strong actual project imagery, a concise hook and clear editorial hierarchy.
- **Supporting cards:** Add substance without turning the story into a text-heavy article.
- **Template:** Bill may supply the first master card. AI should then extract a repeatable production system, test it and refine it under Bill's direction. **No design has been approved or locked.**
- **Media rights:** AI should identify provenance, reuse permissions and feasible alternatives. Attribution is not permission. An AI-generated concept image must not be passed off as documentary evidence.

## Success measures — to be tested

- Percentage of the 18 candidates Bill actually wants to keep.
- Quality of links, verification and visual options; avoid recycled or invented stories.
- Bill's time spent reviewing or correcting the output.
- Repeatability of the design/production process.
- Eventually: saves, shares, follows and useful opportunities generated from publishing.
